[03] / Revised visual direction

A quieter kind of clarity.

An illustrated night sky gives the page atmosphere. Measured serif statements give it a calmer voice. The product stays precise: clean sans-serif controls and readable evidence.

Typography / Fontshare

AaFollow the evidence.

Sentient + Switzer

Sentient 400 brings a confident, readable serif to the large statements. Switzer 400–500 handles reading and UI. IBM Plex Mono keeps exact evidence distinct. Telma 500 gives the wordmark a distinct calligraphic voice beside the winged symbol.

Sentient Switzer

Palette / less color, more character

Night#11121A
Cloud#242335
Pearl#EEEDF3
Violet#BDA4FF

Violet marks primary actions and small signal details. The supplied cloud artwork supplies depth, while flat evidence surfaces remain readable. Success, warnings, and errors remain separate labeled states.
